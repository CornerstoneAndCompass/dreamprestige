FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who drives the supercars?
Self-drive experience for two individuals with hosts leading in separate vehicles, providing guidance via two-way radios. You're behind the wheel the entire time.
Which supercars are available?
Guaranteed: Lamborghini Huracan V10 and Lotus Emira V6 Supercharged. Participants drive at least two to four vehicles depending on the package.
What is the minimum age requirement?
Drivers must be 25 years or older with a full Australian driver licence and no driving offences.
Is a security deposit required?
In contrast to other car rental services, we don't ask for a security deposit or bond.
What about insurance?
Comprehensive insurance is included. The driver is responsible for AUD$15,000 excess for at-fault accidents. Additional excess reduction is available.
What happens in bad weather?
Events may be postponed due to adverse weather conditions. Participants can transfer to future event dates at no additional cost.
